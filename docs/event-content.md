## Proposed public copy

All copy below is suggested wording, not an additional event commitment.

### Hero

**AZINHACK '26**

**What if becomes what's next.**

24 hours. One open brief. Your next big idea.

A national hackathon by IoSC at GGSIPU USAR. Choose a problem, bring your team together, and build something worth showing.

**21–22 October 2026 · GGSIPU East Delhi Campus**

- Primary CTA: **Register for AZINHACK** — opens the official Unstop event page.
- Supporting status: **Official event page on Unstop.**
- Secondary CTA: **Explore the challenge**.

### About

**A little chaos. A lot of possibility.**

AZINHACK brings curious builders together for an overnight sprint of ideas, problem solving and collaboration. Turn a problem you care about into a working prototype — and leave with something to keep building.

### Challenge

**One track. Every possibility.**

Open Innovation means the problem is yours to choose. Build a useful solution in a domain you care about, with TinyFish integrated into the project.

Optional inspiration labels: **Everyday life · A better planet · Smarter systems · Your wild card**. Present these as inspiration, not separate competition tracks or prize categories.

### TinyFish

**Your idea. The live web.**

TinyFish is the title sponsor of AZINHACK '26. Every project must include a TinyFish integration. Explore how live-web information or a useful website workflow can become part of your solution.

Resource link supplied by the event context: [TinyFish quick start](https://docs.tinyfish.ai/quick-start). Verify current links and product wording before launch; do not promise event API credits or free access yet.

### Prizes

**₹1,00,000**

Total prize pool. Final prize distribution will be announced by the organizers.

Do not invent first, second or third prize amounts, additional bounties, swag, certificates or other entitlements.

### Programme

**From first thought to final demo.**

1. **Arrive** — Check in and settle into the build.
2. **Kick off** — Get the brief and understand the TinyFish requirement.
3. **Build** — Make the first prototype work.
4. **Iterate** — Test assumptions and refine the experience.
5. **Demo** — Show the problem, the solution and TinyFish's role.
6. **Celebrate** — Meet the community and take your idea further.

Status label: **Detailed schedule to be announced.** These labels avoid turning conflicting proposal times into public promises.

### Gallery

**A glimpse of the build.**

Photo captions and edition labels should come from the organizer. The supplied image folder can seed an expandable gallery, but do not label the photos as AZINHACK '25 or any specific year without confirmation.

## Tentative schedule — reference only

The proposal plans check-in on the morning of 21 October, a noon build start, overnight work and demonstrations on 22 October. The website can show the untimed participant journey above until the approved programme is provided.

Conflicts to resolve before publishing times:

- Noon on Day 1 to 10:30 AM on Day 2 is 22.5 hours; the stated event window is 24 hours, noon to noon.
- The closing ceremony is listed as 4:00–5:30 PM in one section and 3:00–5:00 PM in another.
- Classroom presentations are listed as noon–3:00 PM, while the detailed schedule uses noon–2:00 PM for demos and 2:00–3:00 PM for deliberation.
- General access ends at 6:00 PM, while overnight access controls begin at 8:00 PM.
- The first mentor round mentions intermediate “results”; no elimination process is specified.
